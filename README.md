## About

I hold a First Class Honours degree in Computer Science (Software Development) and received the Bloc Digital Innovation Award in 2024. My work combines software development, computing education and accessible digital experiences.

I currently work at **Chilwell School**, having joined in **July 2026**. Previously, I delivered sole Computing provision across KS3 to A Level at Abbotsholme School, from January 2025 to June 2026.
